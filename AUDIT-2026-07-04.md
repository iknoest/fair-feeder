## Health

The model layer is rotting: every trained model (v13 Jan, v14 Mar, v15-candidate May) predates or excludes the Logitech domain, so the swapped camera runs on out-of-domain weights and accuracy has collapsed by the owner's own account.
**Financial:** Low cash burn (free tiers), but the flywheel's real currency is owner attention — flag review, label correction, retrain decisions, validation bookkeeping — and that account is overdrawn.
**Strategic:** Y still matters daily (two cats, every morning). The current approach fails the owner's own success metric: a system whose accuracy is restored only by manual labeling is not zero-maintenance; it is a labeling subscription paid in personal time.

## Pivot Recommendations

**Option A (recommended) — Zero-shot VLM keyframe architecture.** Keep the Pi recorder, CI scheduling, Telegram delivery, and clip/keyframe selection exactly as they are; replace custom-YOLO analytics with a vision API (e.g. Claude, GPT-4o) called on ~10–30 keyframes per feeding window to answer the questions the report actually asks: which cat is at the bowl, did it eat, roughly how much kibble remains. This eliminates labeling, retraining, Roboflow review, and model bookkeeping permanently, at an estimated cost of cents per day. Honest caveat: VLMs are unreliable at counting dozens of individual kibble pieces per-piece — but the Telegram verdict ("Dan finished / top up ~N") needs coarse bowl-state estimation, not per-piece bounding boxes; validate this in a 1–2 day spike before committing.

**Option B (only if API cost/privacy is a hard blocker) — One-shot auto-labeled retrain.** Use a large model or Roboflow's auto-label to pseudo-label the already-uploaded Logitech frames, human-spot-check a small sample, retrain v16 once, and freeze. This fixes the domain shift without manual labeling but keeps the flywheel's maintenance surface alive — it treats the symptom and preserves the disease.

**Buy-a-gadget disclosure (Prime Question honesty):** the cheapest solve for *fair feeding* today is arguably a microchip-selective feeder per cat (~€150–300 each), which enforces fairness physically with zero ML. The pivot is justified only because the hardware is sunk and deployed, the delivery pipeline is proven, and Option A's marginal cost is near zero — if Option A's spike fails, the gadget answer returns to the table.

## Board Mandate

- **Continue:** The Pi-recorder → CI → Telegram delivery skeleton — it is verified, unattended, and is the part of the system that actually achieves "zero-maintenance."
- **Stop:** All data-flywheel activity immediately — flag review, Roboflow curation, the V15 holdout comparison, and any retrain planning — since it feeds a labeling process the owner has stated will never happen.
- **Change:** Replace the custom-model analytics layer with zero-shot VLM inference on selected keyframes, making owner-attention cost the primary metric for every future architecture decision.
- **Next Strategic Move:** Run a short spike proving a VLM can deliver correct cat-identity and coarse kibble verdicts on real Logitech keyframes, then swap it into the existing report pipeline.
- **Forbidden Next Move:** The moving Pi 5 camera robot — a shopping list already exists while the core system misidentifies cats, and adding SLAM, motors, and batteries to an unsolved perception problem multiplies the maintenance burden the owner is trying to eliminate.
- **Re-audit Trigger:** 2026-08-15, or the moment the VLM spike fails its accuracy check, or any purchase from the robot shopping list — whichever comes first.

## Must-Take-Away Lesson

A "self-improving" ML pipeline whose improvement loop requires manual labor the owner will never supply is not automation — it is a backlog generator, and the honest fix is to buy intelligence per-frame rather than manufacture it.

## Human Leadership Gaps

1. **Capacity denial at design time (pattern, not incident).** The data flywheel (Phases C1–C4, `flagging.py`, `roboflow_upload.py`, batch review, monthly retrain protocol) was architected around recurring human labeling — a resource the owner now states was never available. The constraint was knowable before the flywheel was built; the system was designed for a more available owner than the one who exists.
2. **Scope expansion while the core degrades.** A second camera was integrated (lessons 63–66, dual-camera CI matrix) without any plan for the domain shift it would cause, and an untracked robot shopping list (`docs/movable-monitor-shopping-list.md`) was authored during the very month the owner describes accuracy as "tanked." Three expansions, zero of which restored the core metric.
3. **Measurement governance drift.** `docs/MODELS.md` records that v13/v14/v15 metrics are mutually non-comparable due to shifting validation splits — meaning for two months the project could not answer "is the model getting better?" with a number, while retraining continued anyway.

These are governance patterns, not character findings. If follow-up is wanted: recommend a separate executive-growth protocol.
